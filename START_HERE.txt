JOURNEY WITHIN / @journey.within.official

Extract this starter ZIP first. Double-click Download-Journey-Within.cmd.
Keep download-manifest.json beside the CMD file.
The downloader retrieves 16 parts (29.3 GB), verifies them, joins the full ZIP and removes its temporary parts after successful verification. Allow 65 GB free on an NTFS/exFAT drive.

When finished, right-click Journey_Within_Windows_Package.zip and choose Extract All, then open index.html in the extracted folder.

If interrupted, run the downloader again; completed, verified parts are reused.

Manual download: https://github.com/KanishkThakar/journey-within-downloads/releases/tag/v1.0.0

See README.md for complete scope, review notes and rights information.
